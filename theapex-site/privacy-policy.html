Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how Tyger Markets Inc ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares personal information when you use our website, trading platform, and social features (the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: When you create an account or use our Services, we may collect information such as your name, email address, contact details, and other information you voluntarily provide.
  • Third-Party Authentication Data: If you sign in or connect your account using Google, GitHub, or similar services, we may collect profile information (such as your name, email address, and profile photo), unique user IDs, and authentication tokens (e.g., OAuth access and refresh tokens) to enable secure login and integrated features.
  • Google Data and API Access: If you choose to connect your Google account, we may request access to Google services such as Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Slides, YouTube, and Photos. Access is limited to the specific features you explicitly enable (for example, importing or linking trading documents, spreadsheets, calendar events, or notifications). We may store access tokens, refresh tokens, and limited content or metadata only as necessary to provide these features.

    We do not use Google user data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or training artificial intelligence or machine learning models. We do not sell Google user data and do not share it with third parties except as necessary to operate the requested features or comply with legal obligations.
  • GitHub Repository Data: When you connect your GitHub account, we may access your public profile and repositories solely to support enabled features such as social trading, code analysis, bot uploads, or strategy sharing. We do not access, modify, or store your code unless required for the specific feature you activate, and you control what repositories or data are connected.
  • Usage Data: We may collect information about how you interact with our Services, including device information, browser type, IP address, log data, and usage patterns, for security, analytics, and service improvement purposes.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We use cookies and similar technologies to enable core functionality, enhance user experience, support security, and analyze usage trends. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, maintain, and improve our trading, social, and integrated features
  • To authenticate users and manage accounts
  • To enable and sync third-party integrations you explicitly connect
  • To communicate with you about updates, features, security alerts, or important notices
  • To respond to support requests and feedback
  • To monitor usage, analyze trends, prevent fraud, and protect platform security

3. How We Share Information

  • With trusted service providers and vendors who perform services on our behalf and are contractually bound to protect your information
  • To comply with applicable laws, regulations, subpoenas, court orders, or lawful government requests
  • In connection with a business transaction such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • With your consent or at your direction, such as when you choose to make content public within the platform or social features

4. Data Retention

We retain personal information and connected third-party data only for as long as necessary to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You may update, correct, or delete your account information by contacting us. You can disconnect third-party integrations (such as Google or GitHub) at any time through your account settings or by revoking access directly with the provider.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, restrict, object to, or request deletion or portability of your personal data. Requests can be made by contacting us using the information below.

6. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in the United States or other countries where we or our service providers operate. We take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption and access controls. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
