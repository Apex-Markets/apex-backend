Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 7, 2026

This privacy policy ("Policy") describes how Tyger Markets Inc ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares personal information when you use our website, trading platform, and social features.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: When you sign up or use our services, we may collect your name, email address, contact information, and other details you voluntarily provide.
  • Third-Party Authentication Data: If you sign in or connect your account using Google, GitHub, or similar services, we may collect your profile information (including email address, name, avatar), authentication tokens (such as OAuth or access tokens), and unique user IDs to provide login and integrated features.
  • Google Data and API Access: If you connect your Google account, we may request permission to access Google services such as Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Slides, YouTube, and Photos. Access is solely for features you engage with (for example: linking, importing, or sharing trading documents, calendar events, or relevant files). We may store access tokens, refresh tokens, and some content (such as metadata about your files, spreadsheets, documents, events, emails, or profile) as needed to deliver these features. We never use or share your Google data outside of these explicit features or share it with third parties except as required to operate the service.
  • GitHub Repository Data: When you connect your GitHub account, we may access your public profile and repositories, but only as required for specific features such as social trading, code analysis, bot uploads, or sharing trading strategies. We do not access, modify, or store your code unless needed for enabled features, and you control what you connect to our platform.
  • Usage Data: We may collect information about your activity, device, browser, IP, and site/app interactions for security, analytics, and service improvements.
  • Cookies: Our site uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience and for functional, security, or analytics purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve our trading, social, and integrated services
  • To authenticate you and enable features via Google or GitHub
  • To display, analyze, or sync Google or GitHub data you explicitly connect (for example: trading bots, spreadsheets, email notifications, documents, social profiles, strategies, or media)
  • To respond to support, feedback, or service requests
  • To communicate about updates, feature changes, or important notices
  • To monitor usage and trends, protect site security, and prevent fraud

3. How We Share Information

  • With trusted service providers or vendors who support business operations (bound by confidentiality)
  • To comply with law, regulation, subpoena, or other legal requests
  • During business transitions (merger, acquisition, or asset sale)
  • With your consent or at your direction (for example, when you choose to make a repo or trading document public within the platform/social feed)

4. Your Choices

You can update or delete your information by contacting us. You may disconnect OAuth providers (Google, GitHub, etc.) at any time via your account settings or by revoking access on the provider’s site. You may request deletion of linked data and content. You may set your browser to block cookies.

5. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

6. Security

We take reasonable and appropriate steps to protect your information, including encryption and access controls, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with the revised date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Policy or your data, contact us at info@theapexinvestor.com.
